We watch the job market for you, always — matched to what you actually want.

Most people only start job hunting when they have to — stressed, rushed, and starting from zero. Career Safety Net flips that: tell us once what you'd actually want next, and (when we launch) we continuously look in the background, keep a standing shortlist warm, and help you stay ready. So if a job ends, you don't start from scratch — you start from a list.

Important, plainly: This is a job-matching and readiness waitlist. It is not insurance. It does not provide coverage, payouts, or income replacement, and it does not promise you a job. Anyone who tells you otherwise about this product is wrong.

How it works

1. Tell us what you want

Roles, places (including remote), your pay floor, industries you like, and deal-breakers you want us to never send you. That's your preference profile — below, on this page.

2. Background matching

Our matching engine checks free public job boards against your profile, scores each listing for role fit, location/remote fit and pay, and drops anything that hits a deal-breaker.

3. A standing shortlist

Instead of a frantic search later, you maintain a current, ranked shortlist of real listings that already fit — updated as new jobs appear and old ones close.

4. Readiness, not panic

Knowing your market, your floor, and your options in advance is the safety net: clarity and a head start, whenever you need it — whether you change jobs by choice or not.

FAQ


Is this insurance?
No. There is no policy, no coverage, no payout, and no income replacement. It's a matching and readiness service, currently a waitlist.


Does joining guarantee a job?
No. It gives you a head start: a profile, ongoing matching when we launch, and a standing shortlist of real listings that fit what you said you want.


What does it cost?
The waitlist is free. Any future paid subscription pricing will be announced before launch — nothing is charged now.


Where does my data go?
On this static demo site, your profile is stored only in your own browser and in the JSON file you download. No server receives it from this page.
